Who We Are

The Department of Data Technology and Analytics develops students' knowledge and skills in data management, technology, and analysis. We prepare learners to use data to solve real-world problems.

What We Offer

Our department provides practical learning in areas such as data analysis, database management, programming, data visualization, and information technology.

Our Goal

We aim to prepare students for careers in the growing data and technology industry through practical training, innovation, and problem-solving.
